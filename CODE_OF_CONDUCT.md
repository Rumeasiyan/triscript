# Code of Conduct

TRISCRIPT contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful, constructive,
and inclusive project community.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- Communicating with patience and consideration.
- Welcoming different perspectives and language backgrounds.
- Giving and receiving feedback constructively.
- Respecting privacy and avoiding real personal data in issues, reviews,
  demonstrations, and test fixtures.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, and
publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community. To report a concern, contact the project
maintainers through the repository's private contact channel. Do not include
personal or sensitive information in a public issue.
